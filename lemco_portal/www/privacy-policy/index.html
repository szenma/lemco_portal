PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 12 August 2026

1. Introduction

LEMCO IKE ("Lemco", "we", "us" or "our") respects the privacy of users of the Lemco Customer Portal and is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

This Privacy Policy explains how Lemco collects, uses, stores, shares and otherwise processes personal data in connection with the Lemco Customer Portal available at portal.lemco.gr (the "Portal").

The Portal is primarily intended for Lemco's business customers, distributors, partners, integrators and other authorised professional users.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the Lemco Customer Portal Terms of Service and any applicable commercial agreement, service agreement, licence agreement, support agreement, Data Processing Agreement or other agreement entered into between Lemco and the relevant customer.

3. Controller and Processor Roles

Depending on the relevant processing activity, Lemco may act either as a data controller or as a data processor.

Lemco generally acts as controller in relation to information required to administer Portal accounts, authenticate users, secure the Portal, manage its relationship with customers, operate support services, manage webinars and training, communicate with users and comply with legal obligations.

Where a customer submits personal data relating to its employees, contractors, guests, end-customers or other individuals to Lemco solely for the provision of contracted services, Lemco may process such data on behalf of that customer as a processor.

In such cases, processing will be governed by the applicable agreement and, where required, a Data Processing Agreement between Lemco and the customer.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that personal data they submit to the Portal has been collected and disclosed lawfully and that all required notices, permissions and other legal requirements have been satisfied.

4. Personal Data We May Collect

Depending on how you use the Portal, we may process the following categories of information.

4.1 Account and identification information

This may include:

  • first and last name;

  • company or organisation;

  • job title or department;

  • business email address;

  • business telephone number;

  • username;

  • customer or account identifiers;

  • preferred language;

  • access permissions and user roles;

  • account status; and

  • authentication and account-security information.

4.2 Customer and commercial information

This may include information relating to:

  • products;

  • equipment;

  • serial numbers;

  • licences;

  • subscriptions;

  • services;

  • projects;

  • installations;

  • warranties;

  • quotations;

  • orders;

  • invoices;

  • maintenance;

  • support agreements;

  • service history; and

  • other aspects of the commercial relationship between Lemco and the customer.

Information displayed in the Portal may originate from Lemco's internal business systems.

4.3 Support and ticket information

When you create or interact with a support ticket, we may collect:

  • ticket contents;

  • technical descriptions;

  • diagnostic information;

  • uploaded files;

  • screenshots;

  • logs;

  • equipment information;

  • correspondence;

  • comments;

  • contact information; and

  • records of actions taken in connection with the request.

Users should avoid submitting personal data that is not reasonably necessary for the support request.

Users must not submit special categories of personal data, confidential third-party personal information or credentials unless strictly necessary and specifically requested by Lemco through an appropriate secure channel.

4.4 Webinar and training information

When you register for webinars, Academy courses or other training activities, we may process:

  • name;

  • company;

  • email address;

  • registration information;

  • attendance information;

  • course progress where applicable;

  • training records; and

  • communications relating to the event.

Some webinars may be provided using third-party platforms such as Microsoft Teams. The relevant third-party provider may process information in accordance with its own terms and privacy documentation.

Where a webinar is recorded, participants will be informed where required by applicable law.

4.5 Technical and usage information

When you access the Portal, certain technical information may be generated automatically, including:

  • IP address;

  • login date and time;

  • logout information;

  • browser type;

  • device and operating system information;

  • session information;

  • requested pages or functions;

  • error information;

  • security events;

  • failed authentication attempts; and

  • audit logs relating to actions performed within the Portal.

This information may be used to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, monitor and protect the Portal.

4.6 Communications

We may retain communications exchanged with you through the Portal, email, telephone or other business communication channels where reasonably necessary for customer service, contractual administration, security, quality control, record keeping or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

5. How We Obtain Personal Data

We may obtain personal data:

  • directly from you;

  • from your employer or organisation;

  • from another authorised administrator within your organisation;

  • through your use of the Portal;

  • through support, sales or service communications;

  • from Lemco's internal customer, ERP, service or technical systems;

  • from distributors, authorised partners or integrators where appropriate;

  • from systems or services integrated with the Portal; or

  • from third-party service providers involved in providing a service requested by you or your organisation.

6. Purposes and Legal Bases for Processing

Lemco may process personal data for the following purposes.

Providing and administering the Portal

This includes account creation, authentication, user administration, access management and delivery of Portal functionality.

Processing may be necessary for performing a contract or taking steps related to a contract and/or for Lemco's legitimate interests in providing secure digital customer services.

Managing customer relationships

We may process information to administer products, projects, services, subscriptions, warranties, licences and other aspects of the business relationship.

Processing may be necessary for contractual performance, compliance with legal obligations and Lemco's legitimate business interests.

Technical support and customer service

Information may be processed to investigate support requests, diagnose technical problems, provide assistance, maintain service records and communicate with customers.

Security and fraud prevention

We may process technical and account information to:

  • detect unauthorised access;

  • protect user accounts;

  • investigate suspicious activity;

  • maintain audit trails;

  • prevent abuse;

  • protect Lemco systems and infrastructure; and

  • establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Such processing is based principally on Lemco's legitimate interests in protecting its business, customers, systems, intellectual property and information assets and, where applicable, compliance with legal obligations.

Webinars and training

We process registration and participation information to administer webinars, courses and other educational activities requested by users.

Compliance with law

We may process or retain information where necessary to comply with tax, accounting, regulatory, judicial, law-enforcement or other legal obligations.

Business communications

We may send administrative and service-related communications concerning:

  • your account;

  • security;

  • tickets;

  • services;

  • projects;

  • subscriptions;

  • scheduled maintenance;

  • webinars for which you have registered; or

  • changes affecting the Portal.

These communications are operational in nature and are not necessarily marketing communications.

Marketing

Where permitted by applicable law, Lemco may communicate information concerning relevant products, services, training or events.

Where consent is legally required, such communications will be sent only on the basis of valid consent.

You may object to or unsubscribe from direct marketing at any time.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Portal may use cookies and similar technologies necessary for:

  • authentication;

  • session management;

  • security;

  • user preferences;

  • load balancing;

  • fraud prevention; and

  • proper operation of the Portal.

Cookies that are strictly necessary for providing a service expressly requested by the user may be used without consent where permitted by applicable law.

If Lemco uses optional analytics, tracking, advertising or other non-essential technologies, they will be activated only where an appropriate legal basis exists and, where required, after the user has provided valid consent.

Where applicable, users will be provided with cookie information and preference controls allowing them to accept, reject or withdraw consent for non-essential cookies.

Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the Portal from functioning correctly.

  • Lemco personnel who require access for their duties;

  • companies within the Lemco business organisation where necessary for the relevant service;

  • cloud hosting and infrastructure providers;

  • IT and cybersecurity providers;

  • email and communications providers;

  • webinar and collaboration providers;

  • professional advisers, auditors and insurers;

  • authorised distributors, partners, contractors or service providers where necessary to fulfil the customer's request;

  • payment, accounting or business administration providers where applicable;

  • competent courts, regulators, law-enforcement authorities or public authorities where legally required; and

  • a purchaser, investor, successor or adviser in connection with a merger, restructuring, financing, acquisition or sale of all or part of Lemco's business, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal safeguards.

Service providers acting on Lemco's behalf may process personal data only for authorised purposes and subject to applicable contractual and legal obligations.

9. International Data Transfers

Some service providers or recipients may process information outside Greece or outside the European Economic Area ("EEA").

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, Lemco will use an appropriate transfer mechanism where required by applicable law, which may include:

  • an adequacy decision adopted by the European Commission;

  • appropriate contractual safeguards, including applicable Standard Contractual Clauses;

  • another legally recognised transfer mechanism; or

  • a specific legal derogation where applicable.

Additional technical, organisational or contractual measures may be used where appropriate.

10. Data Retention

Lemco retains personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and to meet contractual, operational, security and legal requirements.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • the duration of the customer relationship;

  • the period for which a Portal account remains active;

  • applicable warranty or support periods;

  • statutory accounting, tax and commercial record-keeping obligations;

  • applicable limitation periods;

  • ongoing disputes or legal proceedings;

  • security and fraud-prevention requirements; and

  • the need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Account information may be retained after an account has been disabled where necessary to preserve legitimate business records, audit trails, contractual records or legal evidence.

Backups may retain information for a limited additional period until they are overwritten in accordance with Lemco's normal backup and disaster-recovery processes.

Data may be anonymised instead of deleted where reasonably appropriate and legally permissible.

11. Security

Lemco implements technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access.

Such measures may include access controls, authentication mechanisms, logging, network security, backups, encryption where appropriate and access restrictions based on business need.

However, no internet-connected service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and for promptly notifying Lemco if they become aware of suspected unauthorised access.

12. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to the conditions and limitations established by applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • obtain confirmation as to whether Lemco processes your personal data;

  • request access to your personal data;

  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;

  • request deletion of personal data;

  • request restriction of processing;

  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;

  • object at any time to processing for direct marketing;

  • receive certain personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and request portability where applicable;

  • withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent; and

  • lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

Certain rights may be restricted where Lemco is required or permitted to retain or process information under applicable law or where the information is required for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Requests may be sent to info@lemco.gr.

Lemco may request reasonable information to verify the identity and authority of the person making the request.

Automated security mechanisms may nevertheless be used to identify suspicious activity, enforce access controls or temporarily restrict access to protect the Portal and its users.
